Formal proofs in Lean are sequences of tactic steps that transform goal states.
We ask whether these trajectories move between areas of mathematics in a regular,
low-dimensional way. We cluster goal states into microstates, fit a Markov state
model over proof steps, and coarse-grain it into metastable sets with PCCA+.
We compare the resulting sets with Mathematics Subject Classification (MSC 2020)
labels from LLM judges, and use the area model as a prior in proof search.
