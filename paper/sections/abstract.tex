A Lean proof is a sequence of tactic steps, each of which turns one goal state
into the next. We ask whether single proofs move between areas of mathematics.
Two LLM judges label the goal before each step of \resSwitchJudgeProofs{}
Mathlib proofs with a top-level MSC 2020 code; on benchmark statements whose
area is known from metadata they reach \resGoldAccHaiku\% and
\resGoldAccSonnet\% accuracy, against \resGoldMajority\% for the majority
class. Proofs are area-coherent: \resSwitchJudgeHas\% change area at all, and
their steps switch \resSwitchJudgeRate{} times per move against
\resSwitchJudgeRateShuffle{} when each proof's own areas are shuffled, while
the areas of the lemmas that steps cite switch almost as often as a shuffle.
A label-free Markov state model over goal-state embeddings, with a gate fixed
before any label was seen, does not recover these areas (NMI \resMacroNMI{}
against \resFolderNMI{} for the Mathlib folder), and an area prior built from
it leaves best-first search with DeepSeek-Prover-V1.5 unchanged on miniF2F
(\resSearchGainMiniF{} points).
