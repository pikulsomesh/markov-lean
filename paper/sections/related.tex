\paragraph{Neural theorem proving.}
Language-model provers generate one tactic or proof step at a time and
search over the resulting tree: GPT-f on Metamath \citep{polu2020generative},
ASTactic on Coq \citep{yang2019learning}, and HyperTree Proof Search, which
learns from its own proof attempts \citep{lample2022hypertree}. In Lean 4
\citep{demoura2021lean4}, LeanDojo traced Mathlib \citep{mathlib2020} into
goal-state and tactic pairs and introduced retrieval-augmented proving
\citep{yang2023leandojo}. Lean Workbook added a large set of formalized
competition problems \citep{ying2024leanworkbook}, DeepSeek-Prover-V1.5
combined reinforcement learning with tree search \citep{xin2025deepseekproverv15},
and LeanAgent orders theorems by difficulty for lifelong learning across
repositories \citep{kumarappan2025leanagent}. These systems treat the goal
state as text for the policy. We instead model how goal states move through
areas of mathematics and give that model to the search as a prior.

\paragraph{Benchmarks.}
We evaluate search on miniF2F \citep{zheng2022minif2f} and ProofNet
\citep{azerbayev2023proofnet}, and use PutnamBench
\citep{tsoukalas2024putnambench} statements as additional unlabeled goals.

\paragraph{Structure of formal and informal mathematics.}
Premise selection uses the structure of a library directly. Kaliszyk and
Urban mine the inference graphs of HOL Light and Flyspeck for useful lemmas
\citep{kaliszyk2015learning}, Magnushammer retrieves premises with a
contrastively trained transformer \citep{mikula2024magnushammer}, and
Graph2Tac represents new Coq definitions through the graph of the concepts
they use \citep{blaauwbroek2024graph2tac}. Other work measures libraries
without proving anything: dependencies and proof style in the Isabelle
Archive of Formal Proofs \citep{blanchette2015mining}, a minimal item-level
dependency graph of the Mizar library \citep{alama2011mizar}, and the
dependency network of Mathlib, in which about half of the declaration-level
edges cross namespaces \citep{li2026network}. For informal mathematics,
\citet{cerinsek2015network} map the field through MSC codes in zbMATH.
All of these study static structure: which statement depends on which.
We study dynamics: the order in which a single proof visits areas.

\paragraph{Markov state models.}
MSMs summarize long molecular simulations as jumps between discrete states
and are validated by implied-timescale convergence and the
Chapman--Kolmogorov test \citep{prinz2011markov,husic2018markov}. PCCA+
groups states into metastable sets with soft memberships
\citep{deuflhard2005pcca,roblitz2013fuzzy}. We use the deeptime
implementation \citep{hoffmann2022deeptime}.

\paragraph{LLMs as judges.}
Using an LLM to label or grade outputs is now common, and its biases, such
as position and verbosity effects, are documented \citep{zheng2023judging}.
Because no Lean source carries area labels, we use two judges from
different model sizes, report their agreement, and check them on statements
with known MSC codes before treating their labels as a reference.
